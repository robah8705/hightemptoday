WHY ONE STATION PER CITY?

A city's official daily high is the one recorded at its climate station and published the next morning in the National Weather Service's daily climate report. Other thermometers, apps and neighborhoods can read differently, sometimes by several degrees. That's also the number Kalshi's daily high-temperature markets settle on; the beginner's guide and settlement explainer cover how.

Education only, not financial advice. Live readings on this page are unofficial; only the official climate report settles a market.
