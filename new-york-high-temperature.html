WHY CENTRAL PARK IS NEW YORK'S THERMOMETER

New York has plenty of weather stations, but its official record lives in Central Park. That's the station the National Weather Service uses for New York City's daily climate report, and it's the station Kalshi's New York high-temperature market names in its rules.

That matters because stations a few miles apart can record different highs on the same afternoon. The airports sit on open, exposed ground by the water; the Park sits in the middle of Manhattan. If you want to know what New York's high "was," the honest answer is Central Park's number, because that's the one that goes into the record.

THE RULEWhen someone asks how hot New York got today, the official answer is Central Park's high in the next morning's climate report.

CENTRAL PARK REPORTS ONCE AN HOUR, AND THAT CHANGES HOW YOU READ IT

Most big-city stations send a reading every 5 minutes. Central Park doesn't. It sends one routine report an hour, near the top of the hour, plus a special report when conditions change quickly. Of the 24 stations we track, it's the only one without a 5-minute stream.

In practice that means three things:

  • Long pauses are normal. If the New York number hasn't moved in 45 minutes, nothing is broken. The next report simply hasn't arrived.
  • The high can jump in one step. One hourly report can be a degree or two above the last one, with nothing in between.
  • Apps that update constantly are estimating. If a weather app shows a new New York high before Central Park reports one, the app is guessing from a model or a different sensor.

On a typical fall day, Central Park's high arrives in the early-to-mid afternoon. These figures come from the station's own records for 2021 to 2025, counting ordinary days where the temperature rises and falls with the sun.

QUESTIONS ABOUT NEW YORK'S HIGH TEMPERATURE

What is the high temperature in New York today?

The box at the top of this page shows the high so far today at Central Park, New York's official weather station, updated from the National Weather Service's live observations. Those readings are unofficial until the next morning, when the official high posts in the NWS daily climate report.

What was New York's high temperature yesterday?

Yesterday's official Central Park high is shown in the top box and in the 7-day table above, straight from the NWS climate report.

Where is New York City's official temperature measured?

In Central Park, at the station with the code KNYC. New York's official daily highs, lows and records come from this station, not from JFK or LaGuardia.

Why doesn't my weather app match the official New York high?

Your app is usually showing a forecast, a model estimate for your location, or a different sensor, often an airport. Only Central Park's reading becomes the official high, and it reports once an hour, so apps that update every few minutes are estimating in between.

What time of day does New York usually hit its high?

On a typical fall day, Central Park's high comes between about 12:51 PM and 3:51 PM, most often in the 2 PM hour. In summer the median is earlier, around 1:51 PM. About one day in ten, a front sets the high early.

When does the official New York high come out?

A preliminary climate report for Central Park usually posts in the late afternoon. The final report, which carries the official high for the full day, comes out the next morning.
