LAX OR DOWNTOWN? WHICH LOS ANGELES HIGH COUNTS

Los Angeles has more than one official climate station. Downtown Los Angeles has a long-running station at the USC campus (code KCQT), and Los Angeles International Airport has another, right on the coast. On a sunny day they can differ by many degrees, with downtown usually the warmer one.

Which one is "right" depends on the question. For Kalshi's Los Angeles high-temperature market, the answer is set in the rules: the market settles on LAX (KLAX), through the climate report coded CLILAX. If a news story quotes downtown's high, that's a different station and a different number.

THE RULEBefore you compare Los Angeles highs, ask which station. For Kalshi, it's LAX, and LAX is the coast, not downtown.

WHY LAX'S HIGH USUALLY COMES BEFORE NOON

LAX sits a short distance from Santa Monica Bay. On a typical day the sun warms the airport through the morning, then the onshore breeze off the Pacific arrives and the climb stops. That's why LAX has the earliest peak of all 24 stations we track: the median fall high comes at 11:53 AM local time.

  • Late morning is the whole game. The readings between about 10 AM and 1 PM usually decide the day.
  • The afternoon rarely adds anything. By 3 PM the high is set on 99% of fall days. A cool afternoon after a warm late morning is normal here, not a surprise.
  • Inland heat doesn't reach the coast. A hot day in the Valley or downtown can still be a mild day at LAX. Don't use inland readings to guess the airport.

QUESTIONS ABOUT LOS ANGELES'S HIGH TEMPERATURE

What is the high temperature in Los Angeles today?

The box at the top of this page shows the high so far today at LAX, from the National Weather Service's live observations. LAX is the station Kalshi's Los Angeles market settles on. Those readings are unofficial until the next morning, when the official high posts in the NWS daily climate report.

What was the high temperature at LAX yesterday?

Yesterday's official LAX high is shown in the top box and in the 7-day table above, straight from the NWS climate report.

Is the Los Angeles high measured downtown or at LAX?

Both have official stations. Downtown Los Angeles has a station at the USC campus (KCQT), and LAX has its own on the coast. Downtown is often warmer. Kalshi's Los Angeles market settles on LAX (KLAX).

Why does LAX hit its high so early?

The ocean. LAX sits near Santa Monica Bay, and the onshore breeze usually arrives around midday and stops the climb. On a typical fall day LAX's median high comes at 11:53 AM local time.

Why doesn't my weather app match the LAX high?

Your app is usually showing a forecast or a reading for wherever you are, and Los Angeles temperatures can differ by many degrees between the coast and inland neighborhoods. Only LAX's reading counts for the Kalshi market.

When does the official LAX high come out?

A preliminary climate report usually posts in the late afternoon. The final report, with the official high for the full day, comes out the next morning.
