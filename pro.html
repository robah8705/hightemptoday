// THE PRO SERIES · MODEL PICKS

TODAY'S MODEL PICKS

Every morning the model scans all 24 Kalshi daily-high markets and names the brackets it believes the market has mispriced — with its probability, the market's price, and the specific reason, stated in plain words. No reason, no recommendation: picks the model can't explain are tracked and graded, but never recommended. Click any city for its full board.

LOTTERY PICKS

Cheap tickets, honest odds. These are priced like lottery tickets because they are — the model's own probability is printed on every one. Curated daily to the few longshots with the best model case.

RECOMMENDED — NAMED MISPRICINGS

Each of these carries a named reason the market is wrong — a front's timing, a clearing deck, a stale thin book — locked at scan time and graded after settlement.

DEAD AT SCAN

The scan names one bracket per city before the day starts — and never revises it. When a station prints its high overnight, the pick is dead before the scan runs. We don't hide those: they're shown here, labeled, and counted in the record as the losses they are. The trade button is disabled — a dead bracket is not a recommendation.

TRACKED — NOT RECOMMENDED

These made the model's slate on calculator edge alone; no specific market error could be named. They are tracked and graded exactly like the recommended picks — the model's whole record, nothing hidden — but they are not recommendations, and their reason lines say so in the model's own words.
