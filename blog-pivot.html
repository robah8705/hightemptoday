HIGHTEMPTODAY IS PIVOTING — HERE’S WHERE WE’RE HEADED

By Buzz·PUBLISHED Friday, November 27, 2026·UPDATED November 27, 2026·4 MIN READ

Hey everybody, and thanks to all of you who check HighTempToday every day. I hope your Thanksgiving was full of good company and your belly was full of turkey.

I’m writing this because HighTempToday is about to make a pivot, and I want to be fully transparent about why.

HOW THIS STARTED

I started trading weather markets about nine months ago. It was fun. I made some money, lost some money, and stepped away. About a month ago I came back, won my first couple of days, and then lost a lot. The honest truth is I had no idea what I was doing.

So I joined the chat rooms, and a few people pointed me in the right direction. That’s when HighTempToday was born. I wanted one board where I could see every city’s high temperature at a glance. Kalshi didn’t have that at the time. This past week they added temperature cards to the app, which made me laugh, because that was my original idea. I’m kidding, mostly. A billion-dollar company wasn’t watching a site with 200 weekly users. It was probably their next move anyway. And if I was on their radar, that’s pretty amazing.

From there it snowballed. I was keeping all my notes in a document and thought, why not just build a website I could use myself? Then it grew into the Kalshi Agent Playbook, which I tried selling. Nobody bought it. So I trimmed it down, made it free, and made the first pivot: an educational edge.

WHY EDUCATION

We’re all looking for an edge. That’s why we trade, and that’s why I started. And I’ll be honest: I haven’t found one that makes consistent money yet.

What I did find was the same story over and over in the chats: “I don’t know what I’m doing.” “I don’t know what I’m looking at.” “Weather.com said the high would be this.” People get burned, and they never come back. Most of them never learn that every market settles on one specific official weather station, not the forecast on their phone.

So I added a beginner’s guide, tools, and a Start Here page. And as the weeks went by, I noticed something: nobody is really serving this space. There’s no home base for weather traders.

WHY NOW

I believe weather and climate markets are going to grow dramatically in 2027. Prediction markets in general are, and people are catching on. More platforms are adding weather: Kalshi and Polymarket US lead the way, and Gemini, ForecastEx (Interactive Brokers), Coinbase and Robinhood all list weather markets now.

Betting on the weather is fun. Losing sucks. More platforms means more newcomers, and more newcomers getting burned, unless someone shows them the ropes.

THE PIVOT

HighTempToday is becoming the go-to source for weather markets. Think of what Action Network is to sports betting: the trusted place you check before you trade, and the front door for anyone new.

The name stays. HighTempToday is the umbrella, and everything lives under it, including my parody side project PickTheHigh, where Gus T. Winds does his thing.

Here’s what’s coming, without giving away all the secrets:

  1. One daily board for every major weather market across the major platforms. Highs and lows come first, then snow, rain, wind, and eventually storms.
  2. The reading that actually settles each market, with a timestamp on every number.
  3. Education for newcomers: how these markets work, how they settle, and how to avoid the mistakes that burn almost everyone.
  4. Daily recaps: what the high actually was, what the market priced, and who was right.
  5. Graded picks, wins and losses. Every pick on HighTempToday gets graded in public. No hiding the bad days.
  6. A new design, new tools, and a couple of new products I’m genuinely excited about.

CALLING ALL GUEST PICKERS

We’re looking for guest pickers in every city. If you trade your local market, have a decent record, and have a bit of a following, I want you on board. Your picks would be graded right alongside everyone else’s, and we’ll work out a fair share of the pie. This is all informal for now, so reach out.

A SHOUT-OUT

To my friend from the chat rooms, Weather Desk Dave: thank you. He’s pushed me to keep going on this. I doubt myself at every turn, and he keeps telling me it’s a great idea. Dave, I appreciate you.

WHAT TO EXPECT

Big changes are coming to the site over the next few weeks. Some things will move, some will break, and some will disappear for a while before coming back better. If this blog goes quiet or the site looks different one morning, that’s why.

Thanks for being here early. Let’s build the home base for weather traders together.
