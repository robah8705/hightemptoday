THE KALSHI AGENT PLAYBOOK — TRADE JOURNAL TEMPLATE
Companion file to The Kalshi Agent Playbook (Chapter 18).
Copy one block per trade. Paste into any notes app, doc, or spreadsheet.

THE RULES (Chapter 18):
1. Append every trade the DAY it happens.
2. The account's order History is the deciding record — a fill, sale, or
   settlement is real only when History shows it.
3. Never call a day or week FINAL until every leg's settlement row posts.
4. Log deviations as deviations — they are data, not crimes.
5. Correct figures in place, and keep the correction visible.
