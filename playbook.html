WHAT'S INSIDE — THE SYSTEM IN SEVEN MOVES

The full book runs 20 chapters. This is the shape of it — the seven moves your agent runs every day.
1

How Kalshi actually settles the high

The settlement chain most traders never read: the NWS Daily Climate Report for the named station governs, the first official non-preliminary value is final, and the integer part of the decimal is what pays. Trade the number that settles, not the number on your weather app.

2

Peak windows, city by city

When each settlement station actually makes its high — the eastern early peaks, the desert late peaks, and the one station that reports hourly instead of every five minutes. The clock is half the trade.

3

Rule 0 — the agent proposes, you sign off

The standing order the whole desk runs on: the agent researches, sizes, and proposes — a human approves every ticket, in writing, before a dollar moves. No autonomous bot, no standing buy authority.

4

The nightly scout — prompt included

The overnight scan that reads every market and writes the morning slate — and the actual scout prompt ships with the book as a file, ready to hand to your own agent.

5

Sizing & risk rules with hard caps

A fixed dollar cap per ticket, a daily budget, and a price band the agent may not chase out of — set before the first order, not negotiated after a bad hour.

6

Stop-loss clocks: out before the print

Exits keyed to the settlement print, not to hope: when the official number is about to land against you, the plan gets you out first — and the take-profit side banks green before the peak fades.

7

The journal that grades every trade

Every trade logged the day it happens and graded against what actually settled — the habit that turns daily trading into data you can improve. The journal template ships with the book.
